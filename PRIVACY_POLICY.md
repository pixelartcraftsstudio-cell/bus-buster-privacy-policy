# Bus Buster Privacy Policy

**Effective date: November 3, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how Pixel Art Crafts Studio ("we," "us," or "our") handles information when you use the **Bus Buster** mobile game (the "Game").

## Information the Game may process

Depending on the features and services enabled in the version you use, the Game or its providers may process:

- Device type, operating system, app version, and technical identifiers
- Gameplay progress, scores, settings, and session activity
- Crash reports, diagnostics, and performance data
- Advertising or analytics identifiers and related usage data, if advertising or analytics services are enabled
- Information you choose to send us when contacting support

The information actually collected depends on the Game's configuration and the third-party services it uses. We do not intentionally request your name, postal address, or phone number to play the Game.

## How information is used

Information may be used to operate and maintain the Game, save progress, troubleshoot crashes, improve gameplay and performance, provide support, measure advertising or analytics where enabled, prevent abuse, and meet legal obligations.

## Local storage and third-party services

Some game data may be stored locally on your device. If the Game integrates services such as Google Play Games, analytics, crash reporting, advertising, or in-app purchases, those providers may process information under their own privacy policies. Please review the providers and SDKs actually included in the published version of the Game; this policy should be updated if those integrations change.

## Sharing

We do not sell personal information for money. We may share information with service providers that support the Game, or disclose it when required by law or needed to protect users, the Game, or our rights. Any third-party sharing depends on the services enabled in the Game.

## Children

The Game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us using the details below so we can review and take appropriate action. If the Game is intended for children or a family audience, this section and the Game's data practices must be reviewed and updated to meet applicable child privacy and store requirements.

## Data retention, security, and choices

Data is retained only as long as reasonably needed for the purposes described above, subject to legal requirements. We use reasonable safeguards, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure. You can manage app permissions and advertising identifiers through your device settings. Uninstalling the Game may remove locally stored data; it may not delete information held by third-party providers.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the Game or its services change. We will post the revised version here and update the effective date.
